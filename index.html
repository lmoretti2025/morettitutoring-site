Meet Your Instructor

A Message From Luca

Modern tutoring and education has a habit of optimizing for grades and short-form testing that reward memorization over real understanding. I built my practice as the opposite of that: one-on-one, diagnostic, and data-driven, because no two students are missing the same points.

I'm a Northwestern University statistics graduate — top of my class, in three years — and for the past eight-plus years I've tutored across New Jersey and online, raising SAT scores by upwards of 36% and helping over 50 students into the schools they were chasing.

Generic tutoring often settles for explaining the immediate assignment, providing guidance that expires the moment the hour ends. My methods go beyond that: every plan starts with a real diagnostic and understanding, adapting as a student improves, and backed by a private portal — adaptive practice tests, a weak-spot tracker, a running log of every missed question, and more — so we're always working on exactly what's costing points.
