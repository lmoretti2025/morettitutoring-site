Ask a question
Do you meet in person or online?
Online. Most students work with me online, from anywhere in the country, using the same materials and the same portal as in-person students.

In person. Available in Cranford, New Jersey. In-person sessions are held at the Cranford Public Library.

The materials, the method and the instructor are the same either way, and many families mix the two.
How long is each session?
Sessions run about an hour, scheduled weekly at a consistent time. In the final weeks before a test date we can extend or add sessions as the plan requires.
When should my student start preparing?
It depends on the distance between where your student is and where they want to be. Sharpening timing and one weak section can take a handful of sessions. Rebuilding algebra or reading from a standing start does better with three months or more. For most juniors that means beginning in the fall or early winter for spring test dates, and once we have seen the diagnostic I will give you a realistic number for your student.
Which test date should we target?
The first official sitting that leaves room for one retake before application deadlines. We map this out together at the start, so the whole season is planned from the beginning.
Do colleges still require the SAT?
More of them do again. Six of the eight Ivy League schools require an SAT or ACT score for this year’s applicants, and all eight will from the 2027–28 cycle, when Columbia and Princeton join (Compass Prep’s tracker). College Board lists more than 60 colleges that require a score. For this year’s juniors, the score is part of the plan.
Who does the teaching?
Luca. Every session, every week. There are no handoffs to junior tutors and no rotating staff. I build the plan and I teach it.
What if we miss a week or a session?
Tell me at least 24 hours ahead and we reschedule, usually within the same week. The plan pauses and picks up where it left off, so nothing in it is lost. A session canceled with less than a day’s notice may be charged, as the terms explain.
Is sophomore year too early?
For full SAT preparation, usually yes. Junior year is the sweet spot. But sophomores with gaps in algebra or reading benefit a great deal from course tutoring first, so SAT prep later starts from strength.
How much does tutoring cost?
Sessions are billed by the hour, individually or from a prepaid pack, and how many your student needs depends on where they start and what they are aiming for: a few sessions before a retake, a full season from a standing start, Math only, two siblings at once. One posted number would be wrong for most families, so once we have seen the diagnostic I give you a recommended number of sessions and the total cost in writing, before anything is scheduled. Packs are prepaid hours that stay good for six months, and hours still unused after that expire. If tutoring ends early, ask for a refund before then: the unused hours come back, and the hours already used are charged at the single-session rate. The full payment terms, including the late fee, are in the terms. The call and the diagnostic cost nothing either way.
Why not free practice tests or ChatGPT?
Use the free tests. Bluebook’s official practice tests are the best free material there is, and your student will take them with me too. What they don’t tell you is why the score stays where it is: which misses were gaps, which were timing, and which were guesses. AI explanations help less than they seem to, as well: in one study, high school students who practiced with ChatGPT did better on the practice problems and worse on the test that followed. Sessions are built so the method holds up when no one is helping.
