Open as a real account (real data)

  1. Open https://morettitutoring.com/portal/ and sign in as the account you want — for the demo row, that is the Google account tied to key ZQJ9845.
  2. On that page open the browser console and run: 
    copy(localStorage.getItem('moretti_session'))
    That puts the session token on your clipboard. (If copy() is unavailable, run localStorage.getItem('moretti_session') and copy the quoted string by hand.)
  3. Paste it here and open the portal:

The token stays on this machine. It is the same one the live site already keeps in your browser, and it expires on its own. Signing out on the live site does not invalidate this copy — use Forget stored token when you are done.
