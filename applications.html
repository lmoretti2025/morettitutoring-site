Common Questions

What parents ask.

Do you write the essays?

No. The student writes every word. We coach structure, brainstorming, and revision — we don't ghostwrite, and admissions readers can tell the difference.

Do you build the school list or manage the Common App portal?

No — we coach the writing. We don't build school lists or manage application accounts; your student stays the author and the applicant. We make the essays the strongest part of the file.

How many essays are covered?

The personal statement plus supplementals for every school on the list, coordinated so material isn't repeated across essays that shouldn't overlap.

When should we start?

Rolling enrollment through application season — the earlier the start, the stronger the drafts. Ideally by early summer before senior year, so the personal statement is done before school and other applications pile up.

Do you meet in person or online?

Both. In-person sessions across Cranford, Westfield, and surrounding towns; online sessions everywhere else — same method, same tutor. Most families outside New Jersey work with us entirely online.

Who does the coaching?

Luca, every session — and this one isn't just tutoring credentials. He went through this exact process and won it: Northwestern University, top of his class, in three years, Dean's List.
