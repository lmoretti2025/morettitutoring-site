Ask a question
Do you write the essays?
No. We coach structure, brainstorming and revision. The writing itself is your student’s, from the first draft to the final one.
How many essays are covered?
The personal statement plus supplementals for every school on the list, coordinated so material isn't repeated across essays that shouldn't overlap.
What should we bring to the first session?
The school list, with ED, EA or RD decided for each school where you can, any drafts already written (strong English class essays count), and anything already done, such as an activities list or recommender forms. If something is due soon, bring that first.
What if a deadline is close?
Then we start there. Recommender materials, an early application, or a supplement due next week comes first and gets turned around quickly, and the full deadline map follows right after.
Do you meet in person or online?
Both. Online sessions run anywhere in the country, with the same method and the same tutor. In-person sessions are held at the Cranford Public Library in Cranford, New Jersey. Most families outside New Jersey work with us entirely online.
Do you build the school list or manage the Common App portal?
No, we coach the writing. We don't build school lists and we don't manage application accounts.
When should we start?
Rolling enrollment through application season. The earlier the start, the stronger the drafts. Ideally by early summer before senior year, so the personal statement is done before school and other applications pile up.
Who does the coaching?
Luca, every session, from the first conversation to the final submission.
When are sessions?
A standing weekly slot, usually on a weekday evening or Sunday, with extra time added as deadlines close. Between sessions, last-minute supplement questions get answered as they come up.
How much does the coaching cost?
Families come to me with very different arrangements: the personal statement alone, a full slate of supplementals across a dozen schools, a student starting in June against one arriving in October. Those are genuinely different pieces of work, so after our first conversation I give you a recommended number of sessions and the total cost in writing, before any drafting starts. Sessions are billed by the hour, individually or from a prepaid pack. Packs are prepaid hours that stay good for six months, and hours still unused after that expire. If tutoring ends early, ask for a refund before then: the unused hours come back, and the hours already used are charged at the single-session rate. The full payment terms, including the late fee, are in the terms. The call costs nothing and commits you to nothing.
