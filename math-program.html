Ask a question
Do you meet in person or online?
Both. Online sessions everywhere — same materials, same method, same tutor. In-person sessions are held exclusively at the Cranford Public Library in Cranford, New Jersey. Most families outside New Jersey work with us entirely online.
What math courses do you cover?
Algebra 1, Geometry, Algebra 2/Trigonometry, Precalculus, Statistics and AP Statistics, AP Calculus AB & BC, Multivariable Calculus, and IB Mathematics (AA and AI) at SL and HL.
How is this different from generic homework help?
Homework help treats the symptom. A real diagnostic finds the actual gap — often a prior year's material — and the plan fixes that first, so the new material finally sticks instead of sitting on top of a shaky foundation.
Is this only for students who are struggling?
No. Strong students use the program to get ahead of the classroom's pace and stay there — the plan comes from the diagnostic, not from how far behind (or ahead) a student happens to be.
Who does the teaching?
Luca. Every session, every week. There are no handoffs to junior tutors and no rotating staff — the person who builds your student's plan is the person who teaches it.
How long is each session?
Sessions run 60–90 minutes depending on the course and where your student's at, scheduled weekly or twice a week at a consistent time.
What happens after the 8-week cycle ends?
Most families renew into the next cycle, since math support is ongoing rather than tied to a single test date. There's no obligation — every cycle ends with a clear report on where things stand, and the decision to continue is yours.
