FAQ

What parents ask.

If your question isn’t here, ask me directly. I answer these myself.

Ask a question
Do you meet in person or online?
Both. Online sessions run anywhere in the country, using the same materials and the same portal as in-person students. In-person sessions are held exclusively at the Cranford Public Library in Cranford, New Jersey. Most families outside New Jersey work with us entirely online.
What math courses do you cover?
Algebra 1, Geometry, Algebra 2/Trigonometry, Precalculus, Statistics and AP Statistics, AP Calculus AB & BC, Multivariable Calculus, and IB Mathematics (AA and AI) at SL and HL.
How is this different from generic homework help?
Homework help treats the symptom. A real diagnostic finds the gap underneath it, and the plan fixes that first, so the new material finally sticks instead of sitting on top of a shaky foundation.
Is this only for students who are struggling?
No. Strong students use the program to get ahead of the classroom's pace and stay there. The plan comes from the diagnostic, not from how far behind (or ahead) a student happens to be.
Who does the teaching?
Luca. Every session, every week. There are no handoffs to junior tutors and no rotating staff. I write the plan and I sit with your student every week.
How long is each session?
Sessions run 60–90 minutes depending on the course and where your student is, scheduled weekly or twice a week at a consistent time.
What happens after the 8-week cycle ends?
Most families renew into the next cycle, since math support is ongoing rather than tied to a single test date. There's no obligation. Every cycle ends with a clear report on where things stand, and the decision to continue is yours.
How much does the program cost?
Families come to me with very different arrangements: one session a week or two, a single hard course or a year-long catch-up, a student getting ahead rather than catching up, siblings together. There is no single number that fits all of that, so the honest way to get a real figure is to reach out and tell me what your student needs. The diagnostic and its report cost nothing either way, you will have the quote in writing before the first session, and the eight-week cycles mean you are never committing beyond the one in front of you.
