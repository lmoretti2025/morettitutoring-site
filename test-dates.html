Choosing a date

Work backward, not forward.

A test date is the deadline for the preparation, not the start of it. Three things decide which sitting is the right one.

Twelve weeks of runway

The program runs twelve weeks, so start at least three months before your target date. Anything shorter means cutting the plan, not compressing it.

Junior spring is the window

Most students sit the SAT in the spring of junior year, with a second attempt available before applications go in. Junior spring fills first.

Leave room for a retake

Plan the first sitting early enough that a second one is still possible. A score you can improve on is worth more than a score with no room left after it.
