Scope

What we do, and what we deliberately don’t.

Admissions readers can tell the difference between a coached essay and a written one. So can we — which is why the line below is not negotiable.

What we do

Coach the writing

  • Brainstorming — finding the story that is actually worth 650 words.
  • Structure — how the piece opens, turns, and lands.
  • Revision, as many passes as the essay needs.
  • Coordination across the list, so supplementals don’t repeat each other.
  • Deadlines, mapped and tracked with you.
What we don’t

Ghostwrite or apply for them

  • We don’t write the essays. The student writes every word. Admissions readers can tell the difference.
  • We don’t build the school list. That stays your family’s decision.
  • We don’t manage the Common App account. Your student stays the applicant.
  • We don’t promise an outcome. Schools weigh far more than an essay.
Timing

When to start.

Rolling enrollment through application season — the earlier the start, the stronger the drafts.

Ideally

Early summer before senior year, so the personal statement is finished before school and the rest of the applications pile up.

Realistically

Any point in the season. We start with whatever is closest to a deadline and work outward from there.

Deadlines tracked

Early Action, Early Decision and Regular Decision, mapped out together at the start so November holds no surprises.

The promise

We don’t stop at good enough.

No one can honestly guarantee an admissions decision — schools weigh far more than an essay, and anyone who promises otherwise is overselling. What we can promise: every essay gets revised until it is the strongest work your student is capable of, not until a session count runs out.
