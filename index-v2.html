Every program begins with a real test — and a real analysis.

Target pace With return trips Your pace

Finished with 4m 59s still on the clock. The target deliberately banks a reserve for questions that run long and for the review pass — finishing a few minutes early is the plan working. Finishing far early means the reserve was never used.

Unused time

Submitted with roughly 12 of 70 minutes still on the clock while four questions were still wrong. Worth checking whether that is habit rather than confidence.

Second-guessing

Went back to four questions that were never flagged for review, about 38s each. Revisiting a flagged question is the review pass doing its job; revisiting a settled one spends the clock the hard questions needed.

Guessing, corrected for

Of the 76 multiple-choice questions answered correctly, at least 2 are what guessing alone would produce at this wrong-answer rate. The score is calculated as the real exam calculates it; this is a note on how much of it is likely to repeat.

A range, not a point

1450, most likely 1410–1490 on test day. Small samples are reported as ranges rather than false precision — a skill seen three times has a true rate somewhere between 16% and 75%.
